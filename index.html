Smart Grocery Ordering and Delivery Platform

Fresh Grocers provides a simple and modern web platform for customers, delivery agents, and customer service representatives. This system supports registration, order placement, nearby delivery agent selection, location updates, and delivery rating features.

Customer Register Place Order

Customer Module

Customers can register, log in, place orders, choose nearby agents, and rate the delivery experience.

Delivery Agent Module

Delivery personnel can register, log in, and manually update their current location in the system.

CSR Module

Customer service representatives can manually place orders for customers and send SMS confirmation.

Location & Assignment

The system shows nearby delivery agents and allows users to choose the preferred available agent.
